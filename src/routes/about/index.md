Gulp DevStack is a tool to build static websites. It helps you work on your computer, share clean files with clients, and launch your site. All outputs are predictable—there is no hidden build magic or opaque tree-shaking that generates random, hard-to-trace assets.

It is good for:

- Frontend developers who want full control over their code and predictable asset generation.
- Agencies and freelancers who need to send clean, readable code to clients.
- Technical managers who want easy, transparent ways to build and audit websites.

It is not good for:

- Websites that need server-side rendering, client routing, or state.
- Teams that need complex framework tools.
